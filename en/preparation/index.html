How to Prepare
for Your Golf Lesson

You don't need to buy anything or know anything about golf. Below you'll find everything worth knowing before you arrive.

⏰ Before the Lesson

  • ✓
    Arrive 10 minutes early — this gives you time to settle in, change if needed, and start on time.
  • ✓
    Check the location — lessons take place at Krakow Valley G&CC or Royal Kraków G&CC. I'll confirm the exact venue before the lesson.
  • ✓
    Charge your phone — you'll need it for navigation, and during the lesson we sometimes record your swing for analysis.
  • ✓
    Have a light meal beforehand — 2 hours outdoors is quite a workout. A snack before the lesson is a good idea.

👕 What to Wear

You don't need professional golf attire for your first lesson. Just make sure you're comfortable and can move freely.

✓ Yes

  • Polo shirt or t-shirt
  • Joggers / chinos / leggings
  • Athletic shoes with flat soles (trainers, sneakers)
  • Comfortable shorts (summer)
  • Baseball cap or visor (sun!)
  • Light jacket / hoodie (cooler days)

✗ No

  • Jeans — restrict movement
  • Flip-flops, sandals, heels
  • High-heeled shoes
  • Very loose clothing that could get in the way
💡 Tip: Dress as you would for a gym workout — comfortable, sporty, with full range of motion in your arms. Flat-soled athletic shoes are perfect.

🎒 What to Bring

  • ✓
    Yourself and a positive attitude — that's the most important thing! Everything else is optional.
  • ✓
    A water bottle — especially on warmer days. 2 hours in the sun is a lot.
  • ✓
    Sunscreen — the driving range is an open space with constant sun exposure.
  • ✓
    A golf glove (optional) — worn on the left hand (for right-handed players). Prevents blisters. If you don't have one — no worries, we'll manage just fine.
🏌️ Golf equipment is provided! Clubs, practice balls and range balls — I have everything on site. You don't need to buy anything.

📋 What the Lesson Looks Like

  • ✓
    Warm-up and introduction — I'll show you how to hold the club, how to stand, and what a basic swing looks like.
  • ✓
    Driving range — you'll practice hitting at an open practice bay. This is where we'll spend most of the time.
  • ✓
    Chipping and pitching — short shots around the green. A crucial part of the game.
  • ✓
    Putting green — learning to putt the ball into the hole. Satisfaction guaranteed.
  • ✓
    Rules and etiquette — you'll learn the basic rules and on-course behaviour in a relaxed way.

I'll adjust the pace to suit you — no pressure, no stress. If something's going great, we'll move on. If you need more time — you've got it.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

  • →
    I don't have any equipment — is that a problem?
    Not at all! I provide all the equipment. Don't buy anything upfront — see if you enjoy golf first.
  • →
    I have zero experience
    Perfect — that's exactly what this lesson is for. Most of my students start from scratch.
  • →
    Can I bring someone with me?
    Yes! The First Contact Lesson is for 1–2 people. It's more fun together.
  • →
    What if it rains?
    Light rain won't stop us — golf is played in all conditions. In case of heavy rain or a storm, we'll reschedule.
  • →
    Do I need to be physically fit?
    Golf is a sport for everyone — regardless of age or fitness level. No running or jumping required. As long as you can swing a club, you're good.
  • →
    How long is the lesson?
    The First Contact Lesson lasts 2 hours. A standard individual lesson is 50 minutes.
