FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much do golf lessons cost in Kraków? +

An individual lesson costs 120 PLN/h. A couples lesson is 200 PLN. The Green Card course (8 hours) is 960 PLN — educational materials included.

Do I need my own golf clubs? +

No, you don't. When you're starting out you can use clubs available at the club. We'll discuss choosing your own equipment during lessons.

How long does the Green Card course take? +

The course covers 8 hours of individual lessons — theory, rules, etiquette and on-course practice. Educational materials are included.

Where do lessons take place? +

I teach at two courses in Kraków: Krakow Valley Golf & Country Club and Royal Kraków Golf & Country Club.

Is golf for beginners? +

Absolutely! I teach players at every level — from complete beginners to those looking to refine specific aspects of their game. No pressure, no elitist atmosphere.
