FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much do golf lessons cost in Kraków? +

Individual lesson (50 min) costs 150 PLN. Lesson for a couple is 250 PLN. Green Card course (8 hours) is 1250 PLN — with educational materials included.

Do I need to have my own golf clubs? +

No, you don't. At first, you can use the equipment available at the club. We'll discuss selecting your own clubs during the lessons.

How long is the Green Card course? +

The course includes 8 hours of individual lessons — theory, rules, etiquette and practice on the course. Educational materials are included.

Where do the lessons take place? +

I run lessons at two courses in Kraków: Krakow Valley Golf & Country Club and Royal Kraków Golf & Country Club.

Is golf for beginners? +

Yes! I run lessons for people at every level — from complete beginners to those who want to improve specific elements of their game. No pressure, no elitist atmosphere.
